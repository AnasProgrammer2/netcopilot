## NetCopilot 0.18.0

NetCopilot now updates itself.

### In-app updates
- New versions download in the background and the corner card shows the progress. When the download finishes, choose **Restart now** — or **Later**, and the update installs when you quit.
- The card tells you how many open sessions a restart will close, so you can finish your work first.
- NetCopilot checks for updates at startup and every 4 hours.
- Prefer to update by hand? Turn off **Download updates automatically** in Settings → About.
- Works on Windows (installer), macOS and Linux (AppImage and .deb). The Windows portable build, and any failed download, fall back to the installer download.

> This is the last version you need to download by hand. From here on, updates arrive inside the app.
